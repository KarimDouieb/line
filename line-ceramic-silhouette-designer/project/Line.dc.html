1Line 線 — three studies of the silhouette editor

Working core: draw one side of a form freehand (finger / stylus / mouse) — the stroke is fitted to an editable curve, mirrored across the axis, and a family of aspect-ratio variations is generated live. Drag points to refine, tap the line to add a point, double-tap a point to remove it. Heights are in real cm. SVG export works for real; PDF at scale, turning ribs for 3D print, and DXF are on the menus as planned items so they aren't forgotten — same for photo→line input. The three studies vary layout, ink weight, handle style, and how the family is presented.
